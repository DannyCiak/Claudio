# Fact-Finding Question Bank

Use these questions to gather facts before discussing products. Adapt wording to the agent’s style and the client’s state requirements.

## Personal and household

- What are your age, retirement timeline, and household situation?
- Who depends on your income or care?
- Who else should be involved in financial decisions: spouse, adult children, CPA, attorney, investment adviser, or another trusted person?
- Are there health, longevity, or family-history concerns that affect planning?

## Goals and concerns

- What does a successful retirement look like for you?
- What are your top three retirement concerns?
- What would you like guaranteed, and what are you comfortable leaving variable?
- How important are legacy, liquidity, inflation flexibility, travel, gifting, and long-term care?

## Income

- What income sources do you expect: Social Security, pension, employment, rental income, business income, existing annuity income, or other sources?
- When do those sources start, and are they inflation-adjusted?
- If one spouse dies first, what income changes?

## Expenses

- What are your essential monthly expenses: housing, food, utilities, healthcare, insurance, taxes, debt, and transportation?
- What are discretionary expenses: travel, hobbies, family support, charitable giving, and lifestyle goals?
- Which expenses must be covered no matter what markets do?

## Assets and liquidity

- What assets are qualified vs. non-qualified?
- What is already earmarked for emergencies, near-term spending, or major purchases?
- How much liquid cash must remain available after any recommendation?
- Are any assets restricted, pledged, illiquid, or subject to penalties?

## Risk and market behavior

- How did you react during major market declines?
- What would you do if the market fell significantly just before or after retirement?
- Which bothers you more: missing upside or losing principal?
- How much income uncertainty can you tolerate?

## Existing insurance and annuities

- Do you own life insurance, annuities, LTC coverage, disability coverage, or employer benefits?
- What are the premiums, cash values, surrender charges, riders, beneficiaries, and policy purposes?
- Are there replacement concerns or existing benefits that should not be disturbed?

## Tax and legal coordination

- Do you have RMD concerns, Roth conversion discussions, estate plans, trusts, or beneficiary updates pending?
- Which CPA, attorney, or adviser should review tax/legal implications?
- Are there state-specific rules or employer-plan restrictions to confirm?

## Decision process

- How do you prefer to make financial decisions?
- What information do you need to feel comfortable?
- Who might raise objections after this meeting, and what concerns would they likely have?
