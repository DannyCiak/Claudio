# Product-Category Positioning Guide

Use this as category-level guidance only. Do not name or recommend a carrier-specific product without approved illustrations, disclosures, and suitability review.

## SPIA — Single Premium Immediate Annuity

Potential fit: client needs guaranteed income now.

Key tradeoffs: high income efficiency and mortality credits, but reduced liquidity and limited flexibility depending on payout option.

## DIA — Deferred Income Annuity

Potential fit: client wants guaranteed income beginning in the future.

Key tradeoffs: future income certainty, but limited access before income begins.

## QLAC — Qualified Longevity Annuity Contract

Potential fit: client has significant qualified assets and wants longevity protection later in retirement.

Key tradeoffs: may defer income and a portion of RMD exposure under current rules, but limits and rules change; verify current IRS guidance and coordinate tax advice.

## MYGA — Multi-Year Guaranteed Annuity

Potential fit: client wants a fixed guaranteed rate and principal protection from insurer-backed contract guarantees.

Key tradeoffs: predictable rate, but surrender periods, liquidity limits, tax treatment, and insurer strength matter.

## FIA — Fixed Indexed Annuity

Potential fit: client wants principal protection from market loss with index-linked interest potential.

Key tradeoffs: downside protection and tax deferral, but upside is limited by contract terms such as caps, spreads, participation rates, and rider costs.

## Variable annuity or registered index-linked products

Potential fit: client accepts securities risk or product-specific downside buffers and the agent/adviser has required securities licensing.

Key tradeoffs: investment exposure and optional guarantees may help certain needs, but fees, complexity, volatility, and securities disclosures are material.

## Term life insurance

Potential fit: temporary death-benefit need such as income replacement, mortgage coverage, or family protection.

Key tradeoffs: lower initial cost, but no permanent cash value and coverage may expire.

## Permanent life insurance

Potential fit: long-term death-benefit, legacy, business, estate, or cash-value planning need.

Key tradeoffs: lifetime potential and cash value, but higher premiums, funding discipline, policy expenses, and performance assumptions matter.

## Hybrid life/LTC or annuity/LTC features

Potential fit: client wants a way to address long-term-care risk while preserving some death-benefit or contract value if care is not needed.

Key tradeoffs: can solve multiple needs, but underwriting, costs, benefit triggers, inflation options, exclusions, and policy mechanics require careful review.
