# Compliance and Suitability Checklist

This checklist supports licensed-agent sales enablement. It is not legal, tax, fiduciary, securities, or carrier compliance advice.

## Before discussing a product

- Confirm the client’s financial situation, goals, and needs are documented.
- Confirm the task is agent coaching or approved client-facing language.
- Identify missing facts that would prevent a suitable recommendation.
- Confirm whether the client has outside professionals who should be consulted.

## Suitability documentation

Document:

- Age, household, health/longevity considerations, and beneficiaries.
- Income sources, essential expenses, discretionary expenses, and income gap.
- Assets by tax type, liquidity, emergency reserves, liabilities, and time horizon.
- Risk tolerance, market-loss concerns, and need for guarantees.
- Tax concerns, RMD issues, estate/legacy goals, and long-term-care concerns.
- Existing insurance/annuity contracts, surrender charges, riders, and replacement implications.
- Basis for recommendation and alternatives considered.

## Disclosure reminders

Disclose, when applicable:

- Fees, spreads, caps, participation rates, rider costs, surrender charges, MVA provisions, liquidity limits, exclusions, and tax penalties.
- Guarantees are backed by the issuing insurer’s claims-paying ability.
- Indexed annuities are not direct market investments and may limit upside.
- Variable or securities-linked products require appropriate licensing and disclosure.
- Life insurance underwriting, premiums, and policy performance depend on carrier approval, assumptions, and ongoing funding.

## Replacement caution

For any replacement or rollover:

- Compare current and proposed contracts objectively.
- Identify benefits being lost, new surrender periods, fees, tax consequences, and suitability rationale.
- Confirm required replacement forms and state/carrier procedures.
- Avoid recommending replacement unless there is a documented client-benefit rationale.

## Language checks

Avoid:

- "Best product," "no risk," "free bonus," "market returns without downside," "tax-free" without qualification, or guaranteed claims not supported by contract terms.

Prefer:

- "May be appropriate if..."
- "Subject to suitability and carrier approval..."
- "Guarantees depend on the issuing insurer..."
- "Confirm tax/legal implications with qualified professionals..."

## Final validation

Before finalizing, state:

- Key assumptions.
- Missing information.
- Why the strategy may fit.
- Main tradeoffs.
- Required next steps and reviews.
